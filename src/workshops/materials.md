    <h1>Suggested Materials</h1>

    You won't need much to get started -- paper and something to draw & write with is plenty. Having said that, this workshop is a great opportunity to try a new art supply or medium you've never had an excuse to use before! I highly recommend buying from your local art supply shop if you're able. Here are a few of my favorite messy art supplies, in no particular order: 

## Acrylic paint markers
The art store brands include [Posca, Liquitex, and Molotow](https://www.dickblick.com/categories/drawing/markers/paint/), but the relatively new [Sharpie ones](https://www.sharpie.com/markers/creative-markers/) are great for the price. Acrylic markers are more like paint, which means they're wet and drippy &mdash; and will paint on basically anything.

## Pastels
There's something cathartic about scribbling bold lines with a creamy pastel. Student oil pastels are a good choice, but if you're looking for something a little less smudgy that will last longer, I love the [Caran d'Ache Neocolor I](https://www.carandache.com/us/en/neocolor-i-s-1094.htm) (tip: art stores often sell individuals, so you can just pick a few of your favorite colors).

## Keep it bold 
Think about tools that create bold, chunky lines. Finer lines encourage us to get hung up on the details, but we're focused on getting out of our heads, which means we want _broad strokes_. Think fat magic markers, dull pencils, crayons, and even highlighters.
